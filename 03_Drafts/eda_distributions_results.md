## Interest Rate by Loan Status (Box Plot)

The box plot shows interest rates step up progressively across loan status, from Current (lowest, median rate) through Paid Off, Delinquent, up to Default (highest). This is consistent with the grouped means computed in Step 7 (Current: 11.35% → Default: 14.23%), suggesting higher-rate loans were either priced for higher risk at origination, or higher rates themselves contribute to default likelihood — an important distinction for follow-up risk modeling, not something EDA alone can resolve.
